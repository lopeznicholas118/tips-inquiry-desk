AI Inquiry Analyzer and Translator

Description: AI will process and translate inquiries from customers, and a human approves. This will allow employees to save time and focus on serving the customer rather than translating and triaging.

Problem: The business gets inquiries about bookings, lessons, fittings, events, and repairs, in English and Spanish, and staff lose time triaging and translating.

Solution: AI drafts, and a human approves.

Out of scope for v1: actually sending email, payments, authorization
