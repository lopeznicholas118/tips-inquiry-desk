# Fairway Club Miami — Business Facts

## Hours
- Mon–Fri: 9am–10pm
- Sat–Sun: 8am–11pm
- Closed Thanksgiving and Christmas Day

## Services
- Indoor golf simulator bays (hourly rental, walk-in or booked)
- Private and group lessons with PGA-certified instructors
- Custom club fitting (driver, irons, putter) — 90-minute sessions
- Club repair: regripping, reshafting, loft/lie adjustment
- Event space rental for corporate outings and parties (up to 40 guests)

## Pricing (USD)
- Simulator bay: $45/hour, $35/hour for members
- Group lesson (4 people): $60/person
- Private lesson: $120/hour
- Full club fitting: $150, credited toward a purchase over $500

## Policies
- Cancellations require 24 hours' notice or a $25 fee applies
- Members get priority booking for simulator bays on weekends
- Walk-ins accepted for simulator bays based on availability only
